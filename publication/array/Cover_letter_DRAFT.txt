Dear Editors of Array,

Please consider “A Reproducible Benchmark of Receipt Assisted Passive Feedback and Authenticated Routing in Wireless Sensor Networks.” The contribution is a controlled benchmark of ACK-gated censored passive feedback against active proofs and matched controls, together with transparent frozen-policy transfer and emulated MCU execution.

The prespecified 750-run study uses 30 new fixtures. Under blackholes, delivery improves by 4.67 percentage points relative to active proofs (paired 95% interval 2.36–6.97; Holm-adjusted p=0.0161). Matched controls do not establish a unique censoring or attention advantage. A separate 180-run authenticated transfer study likewise establishes no attention superiority. A separately frozen 240-run factorial extension identifies a proof-traffic delivery penalty under always-on listening, with severe low-duty limitations.

A four-node emulated MCU pilot now integrates inference, hop authentication and signed receipts. Q22 inference reduces original signed-receipt deadline misses from 134/200 to 68/200. A separate private source–sink MAC receipt meets the tested source deadlines while retaining deferred Ed25519 proofs; it changes the endpoint and adds traffic. We make no physical RF, measured energy, protected hardware key-storage or deployment-readiness claim.

Code, checkpoints, raw traces, negative results, firmware and audits are available at https://github.com/DrShivamBhardwaj/JICCE_LA_CPPO_Reproducibility.

The manuscript was substantially redesigned following rejection of JICCE-26-0057. The present controlled feedback study replaces the earlier optimization/intrusion-detection framing and does not claim validated live intrusion detection.
