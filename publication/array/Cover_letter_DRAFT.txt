Dear Editors of Array,

Please consider “Controlled Feedback Comparisons and Adaptation Limits in Authenticated Wireless Sensor Routing”. The paper examines whether missing forwarding observations should be penalized or censored, while separating evidence semantics from proof traffic. It is a reproducible benchmark, not a new reinforcement-learning algorithm or a production-security validation.

A separately frozen 1,440-run Cooja factorial study holds ACK gating, expiry, positive credit and late-positive handling constant across four arms. Twelve perturbed geometries include three retained checkpoints, node-ID permutations, both physical attacker positions and benign controls. Post-onset delivery uses geometry-block inference and a prespecified six-contrast Holm family. The active-proof implementation remains a separate reference, and the original 750/180/240/600 studies remain unpooled.

Exploratory reconstruction finds risk and logit changes without relay changes in the historical censored–frozen pairs; controlled offline interventions establish actor sensitivity without claiming radio improvement. A compiler-only O3 MCU pilot still misses 68/200 original signed deadlines. Physical RF/current, protected device keys and reset-safe production state remain unvalidated.

Code, frozen protocols, captures, checkpoints, statistical scripts and negative results are available at https://github.com/DrShivamBhardwaj/JICCE_LA_CPPO_Reproducibility. The manuscript was substantially redesigned after rejection of JICCE-26-0057; it does not retain the earlier intrusion-classification framing.
