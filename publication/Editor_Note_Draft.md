Dear Editor,

We have prepared a substantially redesigned routing study following the reject
and resubmission decision on JICCE-26-0057. The new manuscript examines local
attention, class-specific service debt and online forwarding-risk feedback.
It does not retain the earlier ROA–Maxout superiority claim or the original
classification-accuracy figures.

The reproducibility package is available at:
https://github.com/DrShivamBhardwaj/JICCE_LA_CPPO_Reproducibility

It contains source code, configurations, the historical negative-result records,
new packet trajectories and analyses, and 120 trained checkpoints. A separately
specified 30-seed validation approximately matches comparator capacity, removes
risk information, simulates incomplete feedback, and evaluates frozen policies
under changed node count and attack prevalence. Neither co-primary endpoint
establishes corrected-significant superiority over the capacity-matched MLP.
Risk removal substantially worsens reliability under the tested conditions.

We distinguish the online simulated feedback loop from live intrusion-detection
integration. Distributed telemetry collection, feedback authentication, measured
communication cost and hardware performance remain outside the study. The
revised manuscript and evidence record state these limits explicitly.

The redesigned manuscript is intended for consideration as a new submission,
with the prior manuscript history disclosed. The repository is provided so that
the methods, findings and remaining limitations can be independently assessed.
