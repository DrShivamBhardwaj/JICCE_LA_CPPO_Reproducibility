## Language

Transitions are tied to specific experimental questions rather than generic claims of innovation. Terms such as improvement, adaptation, authentication and validation are qualified by their comparator, endpoint and execution boundary. Emulator timings, scheduled Cooja delays and physical measurements remain distinct. Negative results are stated directly, including unsuccessful O3 optimization and the absence of established attention superiority.
